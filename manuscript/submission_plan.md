# Submission plan — Microbial Genomics

## Target fit

Primary target: **Microbial Genomics**, framed as a genomic methodology / technical resource with a limited public-cohort stress test.

The manuscript fits the journal's stated interest in genomic methodologies, microbial population structure and phylogeography, pathogen genomics, and functional consequences of genomic variation. The journal also maintains a specific collection on genomics, epidemiology and evolution of Campylobacter, Helicobacter and related organisms.

## Required submission-facing components

Current journal guidance requires or strongly expects:

- title page with authors, affiliations and corresponding-author email;
- abstract and keywords;
- Data Summary;
- Impact Statement for most Microbial Genomics article types;
- continuous line numbering in the submitted Word manuscript;
- Funding statement;
- Conflict of Interest statement;
- ethics statement where applicable;
- main text and references;
- sequential figure/table/supplement citations;
- figures uploaded separately in editable high-resolution form;
- supplementary material as a single PDF or combined Excel file;
- public availability of sequencing data and supporting data/code according to the Open Data policy.

## Editorial guardrails

The submission must not:

- describe PRIMARY_TECHNICAL as an experimentally validated phase-variable gene set;
- convert repeat-length alleles into ON/OFF states without locus-specific support;
- present diagnostic pilot p-values as inferential disease-association tests;
- imply PRJNA360417 structure correction was performed;
- state that the central gastric-carcinogenesis hypothesis was disproved;
- attribute the 182-locus callability label to a causal platform effect;
- silently use the stale 44/45 single-country count.

The final disease interpretation is: the central hypothesis remains unresolved because the frozen public data did not provide an adequate discovery-and-replication design under the pre-specified technical and confounding constraints.
